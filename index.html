ORIGIN OF THE FILES

Five signatures. One architecture.

SHIVA is not a required reading order. It is five distinct ways into a connected body of fiction, each with its own tone and audience.

S

CL Seawall

Receipts, witnesses, and the quiet violence of official paperwork.

H

CL Harbor

Industrial dread, buried infrastructure, and large-scale speculative mythology.

I

CL Ire

Memory, history, memoir noir, and the records beneath the stories.

V

CL Vault

Young investigators, unsettling technology, and things found below ordinary places.

A

CL Armor

Protective stories for younger readers, with approachable monsters and useful language.
