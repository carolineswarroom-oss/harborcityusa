Harbor City has a lot of rules.

Some of them are printed on signs, like NO SWIMMING and DO NOT FEED THE GULLS and PARK CLOSES AT DUSK (which is a lie, because the park closes whenever it feels like it and no one argues with a park that has fog).

Some of the rules are unspoken, which is worse.
