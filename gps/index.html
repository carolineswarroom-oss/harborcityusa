About GPS

General Production Solutions.
Exactly what the name says.

GPS works across the practical details of oilfield production equipment: pumps, tubing anchors, tubulars, rod strings, and related components. The work connects shop-floor experience with the realities of field operations.

Whether the immediate need is a pump rebuild, a tubing-anchor exchange, a particular piece of tubing, or help tracking down the right parts, GPS focuses on solving the equipment problem in front of you.
