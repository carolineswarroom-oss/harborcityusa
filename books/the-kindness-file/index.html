RECORD DESCRIPTION

What is on file

In Harbor City, even kindness comes with paperwork.

Dani Reed and her five best friends have survived impossible receipts, stolen days, and a city determined to rewrite the truth.

They thought they'd finally forced the system to blink.

Instead, Harbor City comes back softer.

A new school wellness program promises everything a kid might actually need: quiet rooms, warm tea, free snacks, and adults who genuinely listen.

Read the complete filed description

For once, the help feels real.

But the Harbor Wellness Collaborative isn't interested in the Harbor Six as a team.

It wants to understand them one by one.

Dani is told she doesn't have to carry the record alone. June discovers someone can see the protection beneath her anger. Tessa meets an adult clever enough to get past her explanations. Renee hears the one sentence her defenses were never built to survive. Sylvie begins noticing things about the rooms that nobody else can explain.

And Maris?

Maris actually feels better.

That's what makes it dangerous.

As the six friends drift apart, Dani begins keeping a different kind of ledger. Not what the system does, but what it asks. Not what it takes, but what it learns.

Because behind the comforting language, the carefully arranged support tracks, and the seemingly harmless intake forms, somebody is building a file on every one of them.

And the closer Dani gets to the truth, the harder it becomes to answer one terrifying question:

What if the trap really does help?

THE KINDNESS FILE is Book 3 of The Harbor Six Mysteries, an eerie, darkly funny middle-grade mystery about friendship, trust, emotional manipulation, and the unsettling difference between being understood and being controlled.

Six friends. Six files. One city that knows exactly where it hurts.
