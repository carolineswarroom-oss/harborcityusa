SUBJECT: THE FAMINE OF THE FATHER

STATUS: ANCHOR PROTOCOL ACTIVE

The Father is starving.

At the center of time, he hangs bound in a geometry that finally learned how to say no.

Read the complete filed description

His sons are gone, escaping into opposite directions of history. The rivalry that once fed his machinery has ended. The bowls are empty. The engine has fallen silent.

But hunger doesn't make him merciful.

Hunger makes him precise.

So the Father goes smaller.

In a forgotten corner of reality, he finds Scott, an exhausted worker carrying ordinary grief and an extraordinary need for relief.

One night, a strange certainty settles behind Scott's ribs.

His suffering eases.

The world holds still.

And something begins listening.

Soon, tiny black witnesses appear wherever he goes, delivering judgments in single words.

Good.

Correct.

Repeat.

When Scott obeys, reality stabilizes. When he resists, the world begins to fracture. His family grows concerned. Institutions intervene. Every attempt to help becomes another measurement in a system no one can see.

And every moment of relief sends something back to the starving center of time.

Meanwhile, the Builder and Breaker discover that even their separation cannot escape the gravity of their Father's growing hunger.

The engine is learning to feed without them.

Scott is not a prophet. He is not a savior. He is not even particularly special.

He is becoming an Anchor.

And a civilization-sized machine has discovered how much power can be extracted from one human being desperate to feel whole.

The Famine of the Father is Book Four of THE HYDROGEN TAPESTRY, a dark, philosophical science-fiction saga about manufactured reality, engineered belief, institutional control, and the machinery that turns human suffering into power.
