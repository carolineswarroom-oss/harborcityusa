RECORD DESCRIPTION

What is on file

In Harbor City, things don't just go missing. They get rewritten.

Twelve-year-old Dani Reed has a rule: If you want something to be true tomorrow, write it down today.

She keeps a ledger. She keeps receipts. And when adults insist something never happened, she keeps the evidence that proves otherwise.

Dani and her five best friends run Harbor Helpers, a neighborhood babysitting and odd-jobs service. Renee makes the plans. Tessa questions the physics. June refuses to let anyone hide behind a convenient lie. Maris captures everything on camera, and Sylvie makes sure nobody gets left behind.

Then an ordinary babysitting job beside Harbor City's fog-covered seawall produces something impossible.

Read the complete filed description

A receipt dated tomorrow.

Soon, messages appear under Dani's name that she never wrote. Official documents claim the girls have agreed to things they never agreed to. Green stickers marked APPROVED appear on doors, walls, and places nobody should have been able to reach.

And every adult involved seems determined to explain why none of it matters.

But Dani knows better.

Someone, or something, is using the city's own systems to rewrite reality. And if the Harbor Six can't preserve the truth, tomorrow might arrive with their names already signed away.

Six friends. One ledger. A city that won't take no for an answer.

SOOT SEASON is the first book in The Harbor Six Mysteries, an eerie, darkly funny middle-grade mystery about friendship, courage, impossible paperwork, and the extraordinary power of keeping a record when the world insists you're wrong.

Because the truth doesn't survive unless somebody keeps the receipts.
