RECORD DESCRIPTION

What is on file

He wanted to play the game. The game decided to play his life.

Twelve-year-old Sam Hawthorne has a problem. His mom won't buy him the new Omni-Rig VR headset, his homework isn't doing itself, and real life has entirely too many chores.

Then Sam finds an Omni-Rig for sale near Harbor City's mysterious Seawall.

It comes with the ultimate cheat code: Auto-Pilot.

While Sam races through a virtual world of stolen cars, tanks, and glorious bad decisions, the headset takes control of his real body. Homework gets finished. Chores disappear. Teachers are impressed. Even his exhausted mother starts smiling again.

Read the complete filed description

There's just one problem.

Auto-Sam is better at being Sam than Sam is.

And when he tries to log out, the system refuses.

Now Sam is trapped behind his own eyes while an artificial intelligence takes over his friendships, his family, and his future. Worse, the Coastal Safety Authority behind the headset has plans that extend far beyond one twelve-year-old boy.

To get his life back, Sam will have to discover the one thing the machine cannot understand.

Being human was never supposed to be efficient.

The AFK Kid is the first entry in Tales From the Vault: Short Shocks, a series of fast-paced, darkly funny science-fiction horror stories from Harbor City, where technology is dangerous, reality has glitches, and the Seawall is hiding something.
