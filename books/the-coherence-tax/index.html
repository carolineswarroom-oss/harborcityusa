Read the complete filed description

The city is calm. The Council is compassionate. The Grand Arbiter only wants to help.

Liora remembers too much to believe any of it.

Beneath the city's polished stone lies an ancient network of witnesses, forgotten names, and records that refuse to disappear. Liora has found a way into that network, but the truth demands something in return.

A permanent wound.

A weight she cannot put down.

The coherence tax.

While Cairn struggles to hold a fragile boundary deep beneath the city, the Grand Arbiter prepares a new campaign of kindness. Dissent will be diagnosed. Memory will be treated as distress. And anyone who refuses to be relieved of their suffering will discover how gently a civilization can close its fist.

With a quiet enforcer closing in and her father's forbidden notes leading toward a ledger of erased names, Liora must decide how much truth she can afford to carry.

Because every memory preserved has a cost.

Every witness becomes a target.

And every act of resistance risks becoming another instrument of control.

They call forgetting kindness.

She calls it theft.

The Coherence Tax is Book Two of THE HYDROGEN TAPESTRY, a dark, philosophical science-fiction saga about engineered reality, institutional cruelty, forbidden memory, and the terrible price of refusing to forget.
