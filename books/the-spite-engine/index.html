On a planet without air, a barefoot boy takes three steps and dies.

Then he stands up.

Read the complete filed description

And dies again.

Every death leaves another body behind. Every resurrection sends a signal into the darkness.

When a passing ship finally rescues him, its crew believes it has discovered a miracle.

The civilization waiting for him sees something considerably more useful.

In a world governed by a smiling Council, suffering can be renamed mercy. Memories can be altered. Names can be erased. And the machinery of civilization has become terrifyingly efficient at convincing its victims they are being protected.

But beneath the polished city, something ancient is still listening.

A forgotten network begins answering names that should no longer exist. A young woman named Liora discovers that her family's secrets are connected to the machinery of control. And a boy who has spent an eternity being treated as a resource begins to understand the difference between being saved and being owned.

The Council can rewrite history.

It can manufacture consent.

It can even teach an entire civilization to applaud its own captivity.

But every death leaves a signal. And every signal can become a witness.

The Spite Engine is Book One of THE HYDROGEN TAPESTRY, a dark, philosophical science-fiction saga about engineered reality, institutional cruelty, forbidden memory, and the stubborn refusal of the human spirit to disappear.
