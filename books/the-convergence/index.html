RECORD DESCRIPTION

What is on file

The universe was built to feed its creator.

Then his children learned how to starve him.

Creation builds. Destruction breaks.

For ages, the Father has kept his two sons trapped in a rivalry that transforms every act of defiance into something he can harvest. The harder they fight, the stronger he becomes.

Then a Third Son arrives.

Read the complete filed description

And the geometry of existence begins to change.

Straight lines curve. Flight becomes orbit. Words lose their meanings as the universe translates freedom into permission, identity into function, and resistance into another entry on a celestial balance sheet.

The Father can no longer rule by force alone.

So he invents bureaucracy.

Waivers. Inspections. Fault reports. Emergency procedures. Every new regulation is another trap designed to make his children measurable, punishable, and useful.

Meanwhile, in an ordinary medical unit, a nurse named Rosa discovers that the same machinery is rewriting the record of a dead man.

The computer will accept a classification. It will not accept what she witnessed.

So she begins keeping receipts on paper, even as the words themselves start to change.

To end the harvest, Creation and Destruction must learn to exist without being divided into winners and losers. And the silent Third Son must decide whether he will remain an instrument of his Father's will or become something far more dangerous.

A law that even God must obey.

THE CONVERGENCE, Book Five of THE HYDROGEN TAPESTRY, is a dark, philosophical work of speculative fiction blending cosmic horror, creation mythology, institutional satire, and the stubborn human determination to preserve the truth.

Some rebellions don't overthrow a god.

They make him unnecessary.
