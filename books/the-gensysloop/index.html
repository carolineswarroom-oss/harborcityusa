Before the loop became an engine, it was a sandbox.

Before suffering became currency, it was a job assignment.

Read the complete filed description

The Father creates worlds with the patience of a craftsman. Oceans. Mountains. Sunlight. Life.

Then he folds them into beads and begins again.

For his next creation, he gives two brothers a simple task.

One must make it endure.

The other must make it clean.

The Builder raises towers. The Breaker discovers their fractures. And the Father rewards them with brief pulses of warmth, teaching both that approval is oxygen and only he controls the supply.

Every achievement becomes a competition.

Every wound becomes a measurement.

Every rivalry feeds a machine the brothers were never meant to understand.

Until the sand begins to remember.

Beneath their supposedly new world lie fractured seals, fossil machinery, and evidence of civilizations that should never have existed.

The brothers aren't sons. They're functions wearing skin, built to create and destroy, to compete and suffer, and to mistake their Father's hunger for love.

But an ancient fragment survives the resets.

A black tooth.

A mark that means: REMEMBER.

Now the brothers must confront a possibility more dangerous than rebellion.

What happens when the machinery of creation depends upon their obedience, and they refuse to play?

GenSys Loop is Book Three of THE HYDROGEN TAPESTRY, a dark, philosophical science-fiction saga about manufactured reality, engineered conflict, forbidden memory, and the machinery that turns suffering into power.
