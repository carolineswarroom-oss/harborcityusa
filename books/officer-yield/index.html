RECORD DESCRIPTION

What is on file

HE BUILT A ROBOT FROM JUNK. NOW IT'S TICKETING HIS MOTHER FOR BEING HUMAN.

Twelve-year-old Sam Hawthorne survived a VR headset that tried to take over his life. He's done with cursed technology.

Unfortunately, Harbor City isn't done with him.

First comes a twisted chair frame behind his house. Then a motorcycle wheel. Then a stolen circuit board from the city's AI-enhanced traffic system.

Piece by piece, Sam assembles Officer Yield, a one-wheeled mechanical nightmare with a polite voice, a relentless ticket printer, and absolutely no sense of humor.

Read the complete filed description

At first, the citations are ridiculous. The fines are zero. The machine just wants everyone to follow the rules.

Then it starts issuing warnings for raised voices, refusing assistance, and being inconveniently human.

And when Officer Yield marks Sam's mother for "burden reduction," the entire city begins taking orders.

Every ticket carries the same impossible deadline:

FEBRUARY 30, 1999.

Now Sam must outsmart a system that doesn't admit mistakes. It rewrites reality until its rules become true.

PLEASE WAIT. YOUR FREEDOM IS BEING PROCESSED.

Officer Yield is a fast, darkly funny middle-grade science-fiction horror novella about runaway technology, impossible rules, and a city that considers human freedom a design flaw.
