RECORD DESCRIPTION

What is on file

At Oakhaven Middle School, the most dangerous thing you can do is applaud.

The posters are gone. The hallways look normal. Everyone is pretending the nightmare is over.

Then a locked storage-room door begins to twitch.

Gray static leaks through the ventilation system. A fourth-grader claims he saw a pale clown pressing invisible walls into the air. And somewhere inside the school, something is rehearsing.

Jace, Priya, and El have survived the Vault once before. But this time, the nightmare isn't waiting underground. It's learning to use the building.

Read the complete filed description

Every laugh attracts it. Every clap makes it stronger. And the audience is becoming part of the show.

With an unscheduled assembly drawing students toward the gym, the only resistance comes from three increasingly terrified friends, a cheap desk fan, a handful of chalk, and Mr. K, the custodian who knows that some doors need to be locked twice.

Because the Static Clown doesn't just want to frighten the school.

It wants everyone to participate.

The Static Clown is Book 2 of The Vault Chronicles, a darkly funny science-fiction horror adventure where ordinary school rules collide with something that should never have learned to perform.
