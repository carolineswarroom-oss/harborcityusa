SERIES OVERVIEW / A title-specific summary has not yet been filed.

Every feeling has a name.

Every monster has a counter spell.

Every child already has the armor. They just need someone to show them where it is.

The CL Armor books are picture books for children facing the hard stuff - the moments when something feels wrong and the words won't come. When a scary feeling shows up that nobody named yet. When the adults around you aren't getting it right and your body is the only one telling the truth.

Each book introduces one creature and one counter spell.

Read the complete filed description

The DARVO Dragon shows up when someone is scared of being in trouble.

The Gaslight Gargoyle makes you doubt what you already know.

The Blame Hydra grows a new head every time someone changes the subject.

But CLH has Truth Bear.

And Dad's three fingers.

And a lantern that always shows the path true.

The CL Armor series - emotional honesty for children who are paying attention. And the grown-ups who want to deserve their trust.

Feelings are real. Truth is non-negotiable. Love stays.
