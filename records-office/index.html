OFFICE MEMORANDUM / 001

What is SHIVA?

SHIVA is an organizing system for five literary identities: CL Seawall, CL Harbor, CL Ire, CL Vault, and CL Armor. Each works at a different tone, scale, or reading level. New readers can begin with a single book. Returning readers may notice that the same city, records, and questions keep resurfacing.

The archive's municipal identity is part of the fictional world. Its books, purchase links, subscription system, and correspondence address are real.

SCL Seawall / Mysteries, witnesses, and documentary resistance
HCL Harbor / Harbor infrastructure and speculative mythology
ICL Ire / Memory, history, and literary records
VCL Vault / Young investigators and strange technology
ACL Armor / Protective stories for younger readers

About the records

Published books are treated as fixed records. Their archive pages may later collect supplementary excerpts, corrections, artifacts, and cross-references, without rewriting what appeared in the original edition.

Stories remain readable independently. A connection is not a prerequisite, and an unexplained reference is not necessarily a puzzle that must be solved.
