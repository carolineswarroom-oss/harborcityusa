Privacy notice.

A clear record of what happens when you use Harbor City Archives. Last updated November 2026.

What this site does

Harbor City Archives is a static website for browsing books and related fictional materials. The public book catalog is viewable without creating an account or providing personal information.

Filing Notices

If you subscribe, you provide an email address and may select SHIVA imprint interests. The form is operated by Kit, an external email service. Kit processes subscription requests, confirmations, and email delivery. You can unsubscribe using the link in an email or contact the archive at records@harborcityusa.com to request assistance.

The signup is intended for adults, including parents or guardians of younger readers. It is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Links and outside services

Amazon links lead away from this website. Kit's embedded form may load scripts and process information under its own privacy terms. Those services have separate policies and practices.

Site measurement

No separate first-party advertising tracker or analytics service is included in this version. Normal hosting logs may still be kept by the hosting provider. Embedded third-party services may use their own technologies.

Contact and corrections

Questions about this notice, subscription preferences, or archive correspondence can be sent to records@harborcityusa.com.

The municipal archive is a fictional framing device. This notice describes the real website and its services.
